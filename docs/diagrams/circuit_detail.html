ATmega32A Function Generator — Component-Level Schematics

Symbols: ▭ resistor · ▷ op-amp · ◯ transistor · ▸| diode · ‖ cap. Companion to circuit_diagram.html (block flow) and FINAL_CIRCUIT.md.

A · Signal R-2R DAC → Buffer → U7 centering summer

The ladder's 0–5V is buffered, then split to the scope comparator and to U7, which subtracts 2.5V (via 32k from −8V) to centre the signal on 0V.

B · Output amp — coarse gain bank (CD4066) + offset summing

Inverting summer. Signal enters through one of the CD4066-selected gain resistors; offset enters through R_off; Rf=51k sets max gain ×5.1 → ±12.75V (cannot clip).

C · Dual-rail overcurrent protection

Rs in each supply rail; BC557 (PNP) catches positive-output faults, BC547 (NPN) catches negative-output faults; both LM393 halves wire-OR to PD3. Series R_out + rail clamps protect the BNC node.

D · Oscilloscope front-end (reuses the signal ladder)

Probe protected + biased to 0–5V, compared against the buffered DAC voltage (DACV) by NE5532-B open-loop; the result drives a 6N137 optocoupler into PD2. Internal 10-bit ADC taps the same probe node.
